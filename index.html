HOW TO PLAY

Collect every yellow shard, avoid the guards, then climb the revealed green victory ladder to its topmost cell. Three lives. Ten stages. Good luck!

Controls

← / → walk   ·   ↑ / ↓ climb ladders   ·   ↓ on a rope drop to the next rope   ·   Q / E dig left/right   ·   Space pause/resume   ·   ESC menu.

Digging

Holes close 3.5 seconds after digging, whether or not a guard is inside. Guards trapped early can escape; late guards may be crushed. You may run on trapped guards’ heads, but touching a guard’s body is fatal.

Design on paper

Print the US Letter blank grid at Fit to Page, landscape. Print the US Letter cut-out tiles at Fit to Page (portrait); glue numbered tiles into squares, or write the numbers in pencil. The border stones are already marked 3. Leave empty cells blank or write 0.

Using a photo

Photograph the finished sheet from directly above with all corners visible. Choose Scan Numbered Paper in the editor. The scanner reads the printed or handwritten digits and converts them into tiles automatically, completely offline. The grid is located automatically; yellow-highlighted uncertain digits can be corrected in the preview. For best results, photograph the page straight on in bright light.
